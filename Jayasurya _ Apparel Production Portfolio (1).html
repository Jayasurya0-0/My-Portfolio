Smart Workforce Management System

The idea

Place sensors on sewing machines to capture cycle time automatically, track operator efficiency in real time and record machine downtime. Industrial Engineers would depend less on manual data collection.

  • Clear view of operator efficiency
  • Downtime records for analysis
